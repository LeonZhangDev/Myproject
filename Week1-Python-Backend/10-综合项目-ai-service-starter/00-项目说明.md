# ai-service-starter

## 目标
提供 `/tasks`、`/tasks/{id}`、`/stream`，包含 Pydantic、SQLAlchemy、Redis Cache-Aside、SSE、统一异常、pytest、Docker Compose。

## 建议目录
```text
ai-service-starter/
├── app/
│   ├── main.py
│   ├── routers/tasks.py
│   ├── routers/stream.py
│   ├── schemas/task.py
│   ├── models/task.py
│   ├── db/session.py
│   ├── cache/redis.py
│   ├── errors.py
│   └── middleware.py
├── tests/
├── Dockerfile
└── compose.yaml
```
